Relationship Marketing for Sports Events — the Case of AFCON 2025 in Morocco

A 35-page study of how sports events turn one-off spectators into lasting relationships — built from four international case studies, a review of Morocco's own event history, and my own field survey of Moroccan supporters.

CHAPTER 1
Foundations & international practice

From transactional to relationship marketing, then four events read as case studies — including how each handled saturated social feeds.
